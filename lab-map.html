Interactive Urine Test Strip

Hover or click on a pad below to learn what it detects. The highlighted zone and the matching info card below both light up.

Glucose Pad

Detects the presence of glucose in urine, which can indicate uncontrolled diabetes when elevated.

Protein Pad

Detects excess protein (albumin) in urine, an early indicator of kidney dysfunction.

Blood Pad

Detects blood cells or haemoglobin in urine, which may point to infection, kidney stones, or other urinary tract conditions.

Ketone Pad

Detects ketone bodies, which can appear during fasting, low-carbohydrate diets, or uncontrolled diabetes.

pH Pad

Measures the acidity or alkalinity of urine, useful in detecting metabolic and urinary tract conditions.
