Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Test Name: Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Purpose: To evaluate overall health and detect a wide range of disorders, including anaemia, infection, and blood-related conditions.

Sample Required: 2 ml Venous Blood (EDTA Vial)

Preparation Instructions:

No special preparation is generally required.
Inform the technician if you are on any blood-thinning medication.
Avoid strenuous exercise for 24 hours before the test.

Important Instructions: Carry your doctor's prescription and a valid ID proof at the time of sample collection.

Fasting Requirement: Not required for this test. (Fasting is required only for glucose and lipid-related tests.)

Normal Reference Range:

Haemoglobin: 13.0 - 17.0 g/dL (Male), 12.0 - 15.5 g/dL (Female)
WBC Count: 4,000 - 11,000 /µL
Platelet Count: 1.5 - 4.5 lakh /µL

Report Delivery Time: Within 6 hours of sample collection.

HbA1c (Glycated Haemoglobin)

Test Name: HbA1c – Glycated Haemoglobin

Purpose: To measure average blood glucose levels over the past 2–3 months. Used to diagnose and monitor diabetes.

Sample Required: 2 ml Venous Blood (EDTA/Fluoride Vial)

Preparation Instructions:

No fasting is required.
Can be performed at any time of day.
Inform the technician of any recent blood transfusion.

Important Instructions: Results may be affected by conditions such as anaemia; inform your doctor of any relevant history.

Fasting Requirement: Not required for this test. (Unlike Lipid Profile, HbA1c does not need fasting.)

Normal Reference Range:

Non-Diabetic: Below 5.7%
Prediabetes: 5.7% - 6.4%
Diabetes: 6.5% and above

Report Delivery Time: Same day, within 8 hours.

Liver Function Test (LFT)

Test Name: Liver Function Test (LFT)

Purpose: To assess liver health and detect conditions such as hepatitis, fatty liver, and cirrhosis.

Sample Required: 3 ml Venous Blood

Preparation Instructions:

Fasting for 8–10 hours is recommended.
Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before the test.
Inform the technician about any current medication.

Important Instructions: Certain medicines (e.g. painkillers) can affect results — share your full medication list with the technician.

Fasting Requirement: Recommended (8–10 hours). (Non-fasting results are still usable but may vary slightly.)

Normal Reference Range:

Bilirubin (Total): 0.3 - 1.2 mg/dL
SGPT (ALT): 7 - 56 U/L
SGOT (AST): 10 - 40 U/L

Report Delivery Time: Next day.

Kidney Function Test (KFT)

Test Name: Kidney Function Test (KFT)

Purpose: To evaluate how well the kidneys are filtering waste and to detect early signs of kidney disease.

Sample Required: 3 ml Venous Blood (and a urine sample, if advised)

Preparation Instructions:

No special preparation is generally required.
Stay well hydrated before sample collection.
Avoid strenuous exercise on the day of the test.

Important Instructions: Inform the technician if you are on any kidney-related medication or supplements.

Fasting Requirement: Not required for this test. (Your doctor may still recommend fasting if combined with other tests.)

Normal Reference Range:

Serum Creatinine: 0.6 - 1.3 mg/dL
Blood Urea: 7 - 20 mg/dL
Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN): 6 - 24 mg/dL

Report Delivery Time: Next day.

Electrocardiogram (ECG)

Test Name: Electrocardiogram (ECG)

Purpose: To record the electrical activity of the heart and help detect arrhythmias and other heart conditions.

Sample Required: None – a non-invasive test using chest and limb electrodes.

Preparation Instructions:

Avoid heavy exercise and caffeine for a few hours before the test.
Wear comfortable clothing that allows easy electrode placement.
Relax and lie still during the recording.

Important Instructions: Inform the technician if you have a pacemaker or any implanted cardiac device.

Fasting Requirement: Not required for this test. (ECG is unaffected by food intake.)

Normal Reference Range:

Normal Sinus Rhythm: 60 - 100 beats per minute
Interpreted directly by the reporting cardiologist.

Report Delivery Time: Same day, immediately after the test.
