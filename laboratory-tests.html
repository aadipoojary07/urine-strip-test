Test Abbreviations Explained

Click a term to see its full test details.

CBC
Complete Blood Count - measures red cells, white cells and platelets.
HbA1c
Glycated Haemoglobin - average blood sugar level over the past 3 months.
LFT
Liver Function Test - checks the health and function of the liver.
KFT
Kidney Function Test - checks how well the kidneys are working.
ECG
Electrocardiogram - records the electrical activity of the heart.

Important Notes

Note: Reports for routine blood tests are usually available the same day. Fasting for 8-10 hours is required before certain tests such as HbA1c and Lipid Profile.
